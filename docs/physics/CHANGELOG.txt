I generated four 65-cubed RGB lookup tables from my fixed display, Portra 400 film and Portra Endura paper simulation. The interpolation tables encode stages of the model. The original material profiles were copied unchanged. The tables are derived from spektrafilm by Andrea Volpato, https://github.com/andreavolpato/spektrafilm, and distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0.
